How to matcha

Scoop. Whisk. Pour.

Three movements, ninety seconds, total calm.

Scoop

Two bamboo scoops of Korashi ceremonial matcha into a warm, dry bowl.

Whisk

Add 70 ml of warm (never boiling) water and whisk in a brisk “W” until it foams.

Pour

Pour it over warm or iced milk in your favourite cup. Pure harmony, bold energy.
